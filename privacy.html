Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what data Musignite (the desktop app, the Android app and the web app) handles and why. In short: Musignite has no server of its own, collects no analytics and shares your data with no one.

What Musignite accesses

When you connect a cloud storage account (Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox), Musignite accesses:

  • Your account name, email address and storage quota, to show which account is connected.
  • The files and folders in your cloud storage that you open in Musignite. The app lists folders so you can pick where your projects live, reads the files you open or play, writes files you create or change in the app, and moves files to the trash when you delete them in the app.

Google user data

Musignite requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. It uses this access only to provide the features you use in the app: browsing, opening, playing, saving and deleting your own music project files. Musignite does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not transfer it to third parties and does not use it to train AI models. Musignite's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your data is stored

  • Your files stay in your cloud storage. Musignite transfers them directly between your device and your provider.
  • Musignite keeps a sign-in token on your device so you stay connected: in the operating system's protected storage on Windows and Android, and in the browser's local storage in the web app.
  • Musignite may keep a local cache of files you opened on your device, so they load faster.
  • Nothing is sent to a Musignite server. There is none.

Analytics and tracking

Musignite has no analytics, advertising or tracking. App logs stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Removing access

Disconnect a service at any time in Musignite under Settings > Cloud Storage. This deletes the stored sign-in token from your device. You can also revoke Musignite's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
