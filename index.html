Musignite

Organize your music projects and the inspiration behind them.

Musignite is a desktop app (Windows), an Android app and a web app (PWA) for musicians. It keeps track of your music projects, audio files and ideas, and plays them back.

Your own cloud storage

Musignite can open projects that live in your own cloud storage: Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. You sign in with the provider, pick the folders that hold your projects, and Musignite reads and writes files there on your behalf. Musignite has no server of its own. Your files go straight between your device and your cloud provider.

Google Drive access

When you connect Google Drive, Musignite asks for access to your Drive files. It needs this so you can pick any existing folder that holds your projects. It only lists, reads, writes and moves to the trash the files you work with in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
